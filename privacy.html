Privacy Policy

✅ We Respect Your Privacy

📅 Last Updated

This Privacy Policy was last updated on July 30, 2026. We will update this page whenever our practices change and notify you via the app.

🔍 Overview

Sanctuary: Relax & Mindfulness ("Sanctuary", "we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Short version: We collect almost no data. Your meditation sessions, preferences, and streaks are stored only on your own device. We do not sell your data. Ever.

📦 Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide the app experience:

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Meditation session history (session title, duration, mood rating)
  • Daily streak count and practice minutes
  • App settings and theme preferences
  • Custom sound mixer presets and favourite sounds
  • Onboarding completion status
  • Premium subscription status

This data is stored using Flutter's SharedPreferences package and never leaves your device.

Audio Streaming

The App streams ambient sounds and guided meditation audio from GitHub's CDN (raw.githubusercontent.com). These are public, royalty-free CC0 audio files. No personal data is transmitted during streaming.

Images

Some artwork is loaded from Unsplash (images.unsplash.com). No tracking pixels or user identification is involved.

🚫 What We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Name, email address, or any account information
  • ❌ Location data or GPS coordinates
  • ❌ Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • ❌ Browsing history or cross-app tracking
  • ❌ Camera or microphone data
  • ❌ Contacts or phone data
  • ❌ Any analytics or crash reporting data (no Firebase, no Sentry)

💳 In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Sanctuary offers an optional Premium subscription. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We never see, store, or process your payment card details.

Subscription status is verified through the platform's native receipt validation. We store only a local boolean flag indicating whether you have an active subscription.

🌐 Third-Party Services

The App connects to the following third-party services solely to stream content:

  • GitHub CDN (raw.githubusercontent.com) — ambient audio streaming
  • Unsplash (images.unsplash.com) — meditation artwork images

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them. Neither service receives your personal information from us.

🔒 Data Security

Since virtually all data is stored locally on your device, security is managed by your device's operating system (iOS/Android). We recommend:

  • Keeping your device OS updated
  • Using a device PIN or biometric lock

👶 Children's Privacy

Sanctuary is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App and shared personal information, please contact us immediately.

⚖️ Your Rights

Since we store data only locally on your device, you have full control:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the app (Stats, History screens)
  • Delete: Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local data
  • Export: Not currently supported (planned for future update)
